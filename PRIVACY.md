# Screen Dimmer privacy policy

*Last updated: 27 October 2026*

**In short: Screen Dimmer does not collect, store off your device, or share any personal information.**

## What the app does with data

- **Your settings stay on your device.** The app saves your dim levels, dimming mode, shortcut choices and
  an identifier for each connected monitor (so it can remember each monitor's level) in a settings file on
  your PC. That file is never uploaded anywhere.
- **No network access.** The app does not connect to the internet. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
  advertising and no crash reporting of its own.
- **Keyboard and mouse-wheel shortcuts.** So that shortcuts such as Ctrl+Alt plus the mouse wheel work while
  another program is in front, Windows lets the app watch keyboard and mouse-wheel input system-wide. The
  app only compares that input with the shortcuts you chose. It does not record, save, log or send
  anything you type or click. Input that is not one of your shortcuts passes through untouched.
- **Screen contents.** The app draws a dark layer over your screens. It does not capture, read or store
  what is on them.

## Microsoft Store

If you get the app through the Microsoft Store, the purchase, download and updates are handled by
Microsoft under [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). I, the
developer, receive aggregated sales and crash information that Microsoft provides through its developer
dashboard, which does not identify you personally.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the app removes it; the small settings file can also be deleted at any time (see the
[user guide](USER-GUIDE.md#settings-file)).

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions? [Open an
issue](../../issues) in this repository. Please do not include personal information in it.
